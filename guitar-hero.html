How to Play

Load an MP3 file and press Play. Notes will automatically generate based on the music's beats. Press the corresponding keys (D, F, J, K, L) Tap the colored buttons when notes reach the hit zone at the bottom. Build combos for higher scores! The game analyzes your music in real-time to create a unique experience.
